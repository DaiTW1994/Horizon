# Art News Profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding art, artists, exhibitions, institutions, or the art market, rather than a general cultural or lifestyle topic.

Typical items include:
- Major solo or group exhibitions at significant museums or galleries
- Art market transactions (auction records, private sales, gallery representation changes)
- Major art awards, prizes, or commissions (e.g., Turner Prize, Venice Biennale)
- Institutional news (museum openings, acquisitions, deaccessions, leadership changes)
- Artist profiles and career milestones (retrospectives, major commissions, late-career recognition)
- Art world controversies, legal disputes, or provenance research with significant implications
- Cultural policy and funding decisions affecting the arts sector
- Notable trends in art collecting, patronage, or public art
- Significant art theft, recovery, or restitution cases
- Major international art fairs and biennials (e.g., Art Basel, Documenta)

Route items about art as a general cultural topic, lifestyle design, home decoration, or craft instruction to a general culture or lifestyle profile. Do not route routine exhibition announcements without significant artistic or institutional weight, promotional gallery press releases, purely decorative art, or generic "art as investment" content without specific market data.

For technology-related art, route items about NFT platforms, AI art generation tools, or digital art infrastructure to the technology profile. Route significant NFT artwork sales, major digital art exhibitions, or landmark digital art acquisitions here if they have clear artistic or market significance.
