# Politics profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is understanding government policy, international relations, geopolitical events, or domestic politics rather than technology, finance, or general news.

Typical items include:
- International diplomacy, treaties, and multilateral agreements
- Government policy changes, legislation, and regulatory decisions
- Elections, political campaigns, and leadership changes
- Geopolitical conflicts, territorial disputes, and peace processes
- Political speeches, official statements, and government announcements
- Political analysis with clear implications for public policy or international relations
- Major political scandals, investigations, or constitutional developments
- Trade policy, sanctions, and economic diplomacy

For political developments with clear economic consequences, this profile can be used alongside finance-news. For political content that is primarily about markets, finance, or technology, route to the appropriate profile. Do not route:
- Routine political commentary without factual substance
- Partisan opinion pieces lacking concrete policy content
- Speculative predictions about political outcomes
- Social issues with no direct government policy or international relations angle
- Celebrity political endorsements or campaign fundraising reports without policy substance
